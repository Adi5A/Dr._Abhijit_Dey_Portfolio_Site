About

Brief Biography of Dr. Abhijit Dey: Dr. Abhijit Dey is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Jamshedpur, India. He obtained his B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from NIT Agartala, followed by his M.Tech and Ph.D. from NIT Silchar. He has over 11 years of teaching and research experience, having previously served at NIT Silchar, Madanapalle Institute of Technology and Science (MITS), and NIT Srinagar. His primary research interests encompass advanced surface engineering, thin films and coatings, ceramic-based composites, and functionally graded materials for biomedical, aerospace, and defence applications. His research particularly focuses on the design, fabrication, and characterization of ceramic-reinforced composite coatings, including microstructural evolution, phase transformation, interfacial engineering, mechanical integrity, tribological performance, corrosion resistance, and biocompatibility. He is also actively engaged in developing radar- and microwave-absorbing materials for electromagnetic signature reduction and investigating the machinability and surface integrity of advanced ceramics, ceramic matrix composites (CMCs), and metal matrix composites (MMCs) using conventional and non-conventional manufacturing processes. Dr. Dey has authored more than 90 research publications in peer-reviewed international journals and conference proceedings and holds over nine patents and industrial designs. He has supervised four completed Ph.D. theses, currently guides five additional doctoral scholars, and has supervised seven M.Tech dissertations. He is associated with five ongoing externally funded research projects as Principal Investigator (PI) or Co-Principal Investigator (Co-PI) and has successfully completed four industrial consultancy projects related to net-zero energy solutions utilizing geothermal energy technologies. Beyond his research contributions, Dr. Dey serves as a Guest Editor for three reputed peer-reviewed journals and regularly reviews manuscripts for leading international journals. His research aims to integrate advanced materials development, innovative manufacturing technologies, and multidisciplinary characterization approaches to address emerging challenges in biomedical engineering, aerospace technologies, defence materials, and sustainable engineering applications.
