About

Dr. Abhijit Dey is a dedicated researcher and educator in mechanical engineering, currently working as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at NIT Jamshedpur. He completed his B.Tech from NIT Agartala in 2011, followed by an M.Tech and Ph.D. in 2014 and 2017, respectively. Over the past eight years, he has taught at esteemed institutions such as NIT Silchar, MITS Madanapalle, and NIT Srinagar before joining NIT Jamshedpur in 2024. Dr. Dey is deeply involved in research, with a strong focus on developing thin films, coatings, and ceramic matrix composites (CMCs) for applications in biomedical and aerospace engineering. His expertise also extends to sensor materials and radar-absorbing materials development for specialized industrial uses. Additionally, he has contributed significantly to the study of the machinability of advanced ceramics, CMCs, and MMCs. With an extensive publication record, Dr. Dey has authored more than 80 research papers in peer-reviewed journals and conferences. He also holds over nine patents, industrial designs, and trademarks. At present, he supervises Nine Ph.D. scholars, two of whom have successfully completed their research, along with guiding five M.Tech theses. His leadership extends to five ongoing research projects as a Principal Investigator (PI) or Co-PI, and he has successfully completed four consultancy projects focused on net-zero energy solutions using geothermal approaches. Beyond his research and teaching, Dr. Dey actively contributes to the academic community as a guest editor for three reputed peer-reviewed journals and regularly reviews articles for several international journals. His dedication to advancing materials science and engineering continues to impact both academia and industry.
