Research Interests

Dr. Dey's research spans the boundaries of materials science and mechanical engineering, focusing heavily on enhancing material performance in extreme conditions to address critical challenges in sectors such as aerospace, defense, and biomedical engineering.

  • Composite Materials: Design, synthesis, and characterization of Ceramic Matrix Composites (CMCs) and Metal Matrix Composites (MMCs) for high-temperature and structural applications.
  • Composite Thin Films & Coatings: Functionally gradient and nano-layered coatings (via DC magnetron sputtering, PVD, CVD, and sol-gel) for bio-implants, thermal barrier coatings, and electromagnetic stealth technologies.
  • Machining & Non-Conventional Manufacturing: Machinability studies, wire-EDM, laser processing, and optimization in cutting of advanced ceramics, hard alloys, and composites.
  • Advanced Materials Processing and Characterization: Microstructural analysis, nano-indentation, spark plasma sintering (SPS), and friction stir processing (FSP) of advanced engineering alloys.
  • Radar Absorbing & Sensor Materials: Development of MXene, ferrite-polymer, and nanostructured functional materials for radar absorption, UV detection, and gas sensing.
